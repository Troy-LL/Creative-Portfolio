# Shutter entry, not paper tear

Status: Superseded by [002-fold-open-entry-not-paper-tear.md](002-fold-open-entry-not-paper-tear.md)

## Context

Early spikes explored paper rip / tearable cloth for portfolio entry. That path fights a stiff business-card metaphor, needs heavy simulation, and is hard to iterate.

## Decision (historical)

We entered the portfolio by lifting the calling card like a horizontal shutter over a world behind it. We rejected paper tearing, cloth, fracture, and particle destruction.

## Supersession

The visitor metaphor is now a **fold-open** card (Z-fold / tab pull), not a shutter lift. Tear and cloth remain forbidden; see ADR 002.
